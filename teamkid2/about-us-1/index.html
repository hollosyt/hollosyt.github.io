About TeamKidWhat we believeSupport the club

Know. Live. Share.

Our mission is to teach children to know, live, and share the truth of Jesus Christ. Our vision is that all children at Williamston Community Schools would have the opportunity to have a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

Faith, through a kid’s eyes.

TeamKid exists to mentor youth to grow in their personal relationship with Christ through prayer, worship in song, fun activities, and Bible learning.

We meet on Wednesdays in the Discovery Elementary cafeteria, from the first Wednesday in November until spring break in March. We don’t meet over scheduled school breaks or when school is canceled for weather.

A community with a shared heart.

We are a diverse group of believers—parents and grandparents, homemakers, musicians, teachers, professionals of all sorts—united by our shared faith in Christ Jesus our Lord. By faith we have been saved. Ever grateful for God’s grace, we live to know Him and make Him known.

There’s more than one way to help.

Pray for the ministry, connect with us about where your gifts fit, or apply to volunteer. Community support keeps Christ’s message of hope in our schools.
