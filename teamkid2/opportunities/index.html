Find your volunteer fit

From preparing a snack to leading a group, every contribution matters.

Volunteer with usOpportunitiesExpectationsAdult leader covenantStudent leader covenantVolunteer FAQs

The opportunities and volunteer frequency are flexible. You may play a variety of roles and specify your availability. These roles aren’t exhaustive—we seek to honor God through the gifts He’s given.

Coach (adults only)

Build God-centered relationships with elementary students and Student Leaders through conversation, activities and prayer. Help guide and model good leadership to Student Leaders.

Student Leader (6th - 12th graders only)

Build God-centered relationships with elementary students through conversation, activities and prayer. Fill in wherever is needed: work in small groups with elementary students, teach Bible verses and song motions,etc.

Worship Music Team

Worship music selection and/or vocals – may include movement and instruments.

Administrative Support

Assist leadership with registration and/or communications.

Snack Coordinator

Coordinate snack schedule sign-up.

Activity/Game Assistant

Plan, prep and/or facilitate an occasional game or activity.

Craft Prep/Help

Prepare and/or facilitate an occasional craft.

Set up/Clean Up Crew

Assist with TeamKid set-up (3:00 pm) and/or clean-up (5 pm).
