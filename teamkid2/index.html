When does TeamKid meet?
We meet on Wednesdays, 3:45–5:00 PM, starting the first Wednesday in November and ending in March with spring break. We don’t meet during scheduled school breaks or when school is canceled for weather.
Where does the club meet?
In the cafeteria at Discovery Elementary School, 350 Highland St, Williamston, Michigan 48895.
Who can come, and what does it cost?
TeamKid is for boys and girls in kindergarten through fifth grade. Everyone is welcome, and the club is free for families.
How do I register my child?
Use our online registration form. If you need help, email TeamKidWilliamston@gmail.com.
