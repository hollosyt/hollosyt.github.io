Your gifts can make a difference.

Daring to make a difference in a child’s life just might change yours. Our desire is to see God’s Kingdom come here on earth. We invite others who share that passion to contribute their time, talent, or treasure to TeamKid.

Flexible volunteering options exist. Join us weekly or on special occasions—and everything in between. You may even have a talent that isn’t listed.

Adults & student leaders

Adults can serve as coaches and in a variety of supporting roles. Students in grades 6–12 can help as Student Leaders.

Here’s how to get involved.

  1. Explore the opportunities and find what fits.
  2. Complete the volunteer application and tell us your availability.
  3. TeamKid leadership will contact you with next steps.
