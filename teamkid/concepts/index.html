TeamKid: design concepts

Each concept is the full four-page site with the same words, in a new design. Pick a direction, or mix parts you like.

Round 2: from the design brief

Seven new directions built from the client interview: faith shown as light and joy, easy for a busy parent to register, in the TeamKid T-shirt colours. Light theme only.

  1. Concept 1

    Little Lights

    Every kid is a little light: simple round-headed kids, each with a soft glow in a TeamKid T-shirt colour, gathered on a bright snowy afternoon.

    HomeAboutVolunteerGive
  2. Concept 2

    Sunbeams

    Low winter sun pours through the tall cafeteria windows, and the kids gather in the pools of light. Flat cut-paper kids with a yellow edge of sunlight.

    HomeAboutVolunteerGive
  3. Concept 3

    Suncatchers

    Tissue-paper suncatchers in a sunny window: translucent shapes in the shirt colours that mix where they overlap. Bold and graphic.

    HomeAboutVolunteerGive
  4. Concept 4

    Open Book

    A warm picture book. Each section is a spread, and light spills up from the open Bible onto the kids gathered round.

    HomeAboutVolunteerGive
  5. Concept 5

    Joyful Noise

    Worship in song: ribbons of bright colour flow through the page around big, bold type, with kids singing and doing the motions.

    HomeAboutVolunteerGive
  6. Concept 6

    One Line

    One unbroken line draws the whole site, from kids holding hands to a leader with a book, with sunny highlighter fills. The calmest of the set.

    HomeAboutVolunteerGive
  7. Concept 7

    Team Colours

    It's TeamKid, in its own shirts: jersey stripes, stitched patches, pennants and a sunny scoreboard, with the TK monogram in a sun crest.
