Donate

Thank you!

TeamKid is completely free to attend because donations from generous people like you cover the cost of snacks, materials, t-shirts, prizes, and Bibles. Operational expenses run about $4,000 per year, and if you’re feeling led to give to TeamKid, there are a few ways to do so:

  • snacks
  • materials
  • t-shirts
  • prizes
  • Bibles

Ways to give

  • Zelle

    TeamKidWilliamston@gmail.com

    Donate using Zelle to TeamKidWilliamston@gmail.com.

  • PayPal

    @TeamKid

    Donate using PayPal @TeamKid.

  • Cash or check, in person

    The jar at TeamKid

    Place cash, or a check made payable to TeamKid Williamston, in the jar at TeamKid.

  • By mail

    TeamKid c/o Gayle Cobb

    4400 N Williamston Rd, Williamston, MI 48895. Make the check out to TeamKid Williamston — or, if you would like a tax-deductible receipt, to Williamston Free Methodist Church.
