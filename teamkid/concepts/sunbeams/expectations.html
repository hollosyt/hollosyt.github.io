Expectations

We serve an awesome God. We strive for excellence in all we do.

To that end, volunteers are asked to:

  • Respect all school rules; help ensure this borrowed space looks better than when we arrived.
  • Conduct all student interactions in a public space where at least one other volunteer is present.
  • Instruct students to sit beside you if they try to sit on your lap.
  • Remain outside the bathroom when monitoring. Enter only with another adult if a student calls for help.
  • Put away all electronic devices for the entire duration of TeamKid.
  • Conduct yourselves in an excellent and loving manner – remembering always Who you represent.
