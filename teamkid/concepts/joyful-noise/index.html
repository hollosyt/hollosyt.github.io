Volunteer with us

Free after school Bible club for boys and girls in Kindergarten – 5th grade. We gather in the cafeteria of Williamston Discovery Elementary after school until 5:00 PM.

Who we are

More than an after-school Bible club.

Our vision: that all children at Williamston Community Schools would have the opportunity to have a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

TeamKid exists to mentor youth to grow in their personal relationship with Christ through prayer, worship in song, fun activities, and Bible learning.

  • worship in song
  • fun activities
  • Bible learning
Read more about who we are

When we acknowledge Jesus as Lord and surrender our lives to Him as Savior, we become part of a much larger family.

When & where

Wednesday afternoon, in the cafeteria.
