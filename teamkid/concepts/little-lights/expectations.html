Expectations

We serve an awesome God. We strive for excellence in all we do.

To that end, volunteers are asked to:

  1. Respect all school rules; help ensure this borrowed space looks better than when we arrived.
  2. Conduct all student interactions in a public space where at least one other volunteer is present.
  3. Instruct students to sit beside you if they try to sit on your lap.
  4. Remain outside the bathroom when monitoring. Enter only with another adult if a student calls for help.
  5. Put away all electronic devices for the entire duration of TeamKid.
  6. Conduct yourselves in an excellent and loving manner – remembering always Who you represent.
