Who we are

More than an after-school Bible club.

Our vision: that all children at Williamston Community Schools would have the opportunity to have a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

TeamKid exists to mentor youth to grow in their personal relationship with Christ through prayer, worship in song, fun activities, and Bible learning.
