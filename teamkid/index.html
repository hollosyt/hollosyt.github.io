Who we are

More than an after-school Bible club. We’re family.

Our vision: that all children at Williamston Community Schools would have the opportunity to have a personal relationship with Jesus Christ.

We are a diverse group of believers – parents and grandparents, homemakers, musicians, teachers, professionals of all sorts – united by our shared faith in Christ Jesus our Lord.

When we acknowledge Jesus as Lord and surrender our lives to Him as Savior, we become part of a much larger family.

Our program

What we do.

TeamKid exists to mentor youth to grow in their personal relationship with Christ through prayer, worship in song, fun activities, and Bible learning.

Worship in song

Worship music selection and vocals — may include movement and instruments.

Bible learning

Learning Bible verses and song motions, in small groups.

Fun activities

Games, crafts and a snack, planned and prepped by volunteers.

Prayer

God-centered relationships built through conversation, activities and prayer.

Groups are led by adult coaches alongside student leaders in 6th–12th grade, whom the coaches help guide and model good leadership to. See how volunteering works →
